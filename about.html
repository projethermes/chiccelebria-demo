Chic Celebria curates decor and personalised gifts for Halloween, Christmas and everything in between — an edit, not an aisle.

Chic Celebria brings together decor and personalised gifts for the celebrations that matter — Halloween, Christmas, and the birthdays, gifts and everyday moments that fall in between. We pick pieces that hold up as decor first, seasonal second: things you'd want to keep out, not just pack away in January.

How to shop right now

We're in an early phase: every piece on this site is sold and fulfilled through our Etsy shop. Browse here, then follow the "Shop on Etsy" link on any product to complete your order and message us directly about sizes, personalisation or delivery timing.

Personalisation

Several pieces can be made yours — a name on a tapestry, a message on a candle label, initials on a cushion. Where personalisation is offered, you'll find the details on the product page, with instructions for sending us your text or photo after ordering.

Considered, not crammed

We'd rather show a short, well-chosen edit than a wall of everything. If a collection feels small, that's by design — it grows as we add pieces we actually stand behind.
